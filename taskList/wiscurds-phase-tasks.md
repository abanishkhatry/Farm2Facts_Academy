# Tasks by Phase

The task guide for each WISCURDS phase. Each phase's tasks are published here as that phase begins. For what a phase covers and what you are expected to complete before moving on, open the matching card on [Project Milestones]({{ site.baseurl }}/taskList/wiscurds-key-milestones).

From Phase 2 onward the four students split into **two teams of two**, each on its own project:

- **Project A -- Admin Dashboard.** Works on the platform's own data in the local database.
- **Project B -- Wisconet API.** Works on the external data source and the Market Profile.
